- **Tag:** `v0.10.2`
- **Headline changes:** docs-only patch. The packaged monitoring guide's
  Completion Test now documents excluding the harness-owned dispatch stream
  from clean-tree checks in repos that track `.harnex/dispatch.jsonl`, plus
