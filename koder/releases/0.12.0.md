## Long-run telemetry

No multi-hour or overnight dispatch was run. The three independent review
sessions lasted 468s, 489s, and 662s (1,619s total), all ended `success`, and
reported zero disconnections. Their combined observed token count was
5,803,314. The first installed Pi smoke ran for 20s with 28,077 observed tokens,
`task_complete`, `exit=success`, and zero disconnections; its completion event
recorded `marker_written=true`, `hook_configured=true`, and
`hook_launched=true`. The corrected isolated temp-repo smoke published its
marker at 13s and exited successfully.

The first smoke verifier omitted `--cwd`, so the successful run targeted the
Harnex checkout while the script looked for a temp-repo marker. The known
`RESULT.txt` artifact was removed, its append-only dispatch rows were retained,
and the smoke was repeated with explicit `--cwd`; the corrected run passed.
This was a verifier setup error, not a product failure.

## What this verification did NOT cover

- No multi-hour, overnight, reconnect-stress, or high-concurrency provider run.
- No real-provider rejected/failed/error hook; those outcomes and marker-write /
  hook-spawn failures are covered hermetically.
- No persistent Pi reuse; Issue #69 remains open and fresh `--auto-stop`
  workers remain the supported safe lifecycle.
- No heartbeat or hard-deadline behavior; those remaining Issue #71 slices are
  explicitly deferred to later plans.
- No macOS, BSD, or Windows verification.
